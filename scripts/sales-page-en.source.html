FAQ

Your questions

How will I receive the catalogue?

Right after payment: your personal access code shows on screen and is sent by email. With your email and this code, you open your Premium space on your phone or computer, for life.

How does the cost calculator work?

For each house plan, the catalogue shows how the construction budget splits by item (substructure, walling, roof, doors and windows, finishes…). Enter the price per square meter quoted by your builder, or choose a Basic, Standard or Luxury level: the calculator gives you the total and the amount for each item. Construction prices change from one town to another, so always confirm them with your builder.

Can I build with the plans in the catalogue?

The catalogue is for choosing your house and estimating its budget: we do not recommend building with it. Order the full construction drawings: our architects adapt the design to your plot and your needs. Fixed price: ≈ KES 45,600 for a bungalow, ≈ KES 68,400 for a two-story house. Your local authority may ask for drawings stamped by a locally registered professional before issuing a building permit.

What do the full construction drawings include?

10 drawings, for a bungalow as for a two-story house:

  1. Foundation Plan
  2. Floor Plans: rooms, circulation, openings, dimensions
  3. Front Elevation
  4. Rear Elevation
  5. Left Elevation
  6. Right Elevation
  7. Section AA: levels and heights
  8. Section BB
  9. Roof Plan
  10. Septic Tank Plan

Your plans are customizable: tell us what you need and we modify the design you chose.

How do I pay with Mobile Money?

Choose your network and confirm. A payment request arrives on your phone: approve it with your PIN within 2 minutes. Make sure you have KES 8,892.42 plus your network fees. Cards are accepted too.

My payment failed. What should I do?

If the payment fails, nothing is charged. Common causes: the request was not approved in time, not enough balance, or a weak network. Try again, or message us on WhatsApp: we will help you finish.

Do I pay for new house plans?

No. You pay once: your access is yours for life, new designs included, at no extra cost.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Send us a WhatsApp message within 7 days and we refund you in full. No questions asked.
